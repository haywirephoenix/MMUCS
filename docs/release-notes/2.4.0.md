## Highlights

**The game window.** *Window ▸ Game* (or `MMUCS --game [FILE]` to open just the game) runs The Curse of
Monkey Island from its own scripts, in a new script interpreter written for MMUCS.

- Rooms, objects and actors drawn as the engine draws them, with z-plane cuts, shadows, palettes and fades,
  and an optional smooth camera and smooth walking.
- Walking, the verb coin, the inventory, dialogue, cutscenes (Esc skips) and videos with their sound and
  subtitles.
- Speech with lip sync, subtitles in the game's fonts or a replacement typeface, and Ctrl+T to cycle the voice modes.
- Digital iMUSE music with its transitions, sound effects, and group volumes with music ducking under speech.
- Saving and loading: the F1 menu with save thumbnails, F6 to quick save and F4 to quick load, and
  `--load N` to start from a save.

**Mods.** `_mods/<mod>/` folders, laid out like an export:

- Music mods map FLAC soundtracks onto the game's tracks and loop where the game's own tracks loop.
  There's a new FLAC decoder for them.
- Hi-res mods replace room backgrounds and object images at their own resolution, in full colour,
  darkened by the game's shadow tables.
- Costume mods replace costume cels.
- The Room viewer has a *Mod images* toggle and a Position section to move a mod image or cel and save
  its place.

**Under the hood.** MMUCS.Player draws the running game on any Skia canvas without a UI framework, and
the Room, Costume and Media viewers now draw through it.
